Menstruation Research Conference 2026

jessica yang June 08, 2026
postpartum-psychosis bipolar-disorder awards

In June 2026, Professor Arianna Di Florio and Dr Jessica Yang attended the Mensturation Research Conference 2026, hosted by the Menarche, Menstruation, Menopause and Mental Health (4m) Consortium and the Menstruation Research Network (MRN) in Brighton. Jess presented on her work on the genetics of postpartum psychosis and how this might inform our understanding of severe mental illness at other reproductive time points. She was awarded the Early Career Researcher best oral presentation award.
