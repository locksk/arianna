Professor Arianna Di Florio and researcher Chloe Apsey attended an event with the International Association for Premenstrual Disorders (IAPMD) and people with lived experience of PMDD. Arianna joined patient representatives and lived experience panelists to discuss approaches to treatment for premenstrual disorders.

Read more at the [blog post on National Centre for Mental Health's website](https://www.cardiff.ac.uk/news/view/2741271-breaking-the-stigma-making-menstrual-health-a-part-of-everyday-conversation).
