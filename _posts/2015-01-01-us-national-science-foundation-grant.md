As part of her time at the University of North Carolina during her Maria Skłodowska-Curie Fellowship, Dr Arianna Di Florio was a co-investigator on a project titled "Interactive Ensemble Clustering for Mixed Data with Application to Mood Disorders". The team were awarded $99,998 to investigate big-data methods for improving diagnosis and treatment of mood disorders.
