Prof Arianna Di Florio wrote an article for Neurotorium titled ['Perimenopause, Menopause, and Mental Health'](https://neurotorium.org/article/perimenopause-menopause-and-mental-health/).

Neurotorium is a platform founded by the Lundbeck Foundation which provides free educational content to clinicians, researchers, and any who are interested in neurology.

Arianna's article explains the physiological changes that occur during the menopausal transition and outlines what is currently known about the association between perimenopause and mental health.
