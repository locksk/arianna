### About

Action on Postpartum Psychosis (APP) is a charity that supports and advocates for mothers and families who have been affected by postpartum psychosis. It provides information for women affect by the disorder, and their families, that has been co-produced by lived experience experts, clinicians, and academic experts. It also signposts towards award-winning support and information to help both now and in the future.
