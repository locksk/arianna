### About

The Bipolar Disorder Research Network (BDRN) is a collaboration between Cardiff University and University of Worcester, supported by the Wellcome Trust and the Stanley Medical Research Institute. This is the largest individual research programme focussing on bipolar disorder, with over 7500 people having participated.
