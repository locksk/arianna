Womens Winter Webinar Series

arianna di florio and jessica yang and lisa shitomi-jones and chloe apsey March 25, 2024 July 28, 2026
postpartum-psychosis pmdd perimenopause research webinar

In the winter months of 2023/2024, the team organised the Women’s Winter Webinar series, focusing on women’s mental health across the lifespan.

This webinar series discussed how reproductive events such as pregnancy, the menstrual cycle and reproductive ageing impact the mental health of women and people assigned female at birth (AFAB). Each session of the series detailed what we know about mental health research in these areas already and what research the team has been working on recently. The PMDD webinar also saw the launch of the new PMDD animation which was developed in collaboration with people with lived experience of PMDD.
